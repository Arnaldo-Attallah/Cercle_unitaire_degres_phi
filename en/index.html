When the angle varies, it is ωt; when it is stopped, it is ϕ. This page shows the fixed angle ϕ, in degrees: choose a special angle or move the slider (0 to 90°) to read the six functions of ϕ. In a right triangle, ϕ goes from 0 to 90°: the figure therefore shows the first quarter of the circle, enlarged. To see the angle ωt vary in radians, open the radians version (ωt).
